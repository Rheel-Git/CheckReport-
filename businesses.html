Common questions

Will my profile be public as soon as I submit it?

No. New business profiles remain pending until an administrator approves them.

How do I ask customers to review my business?

After approval, share your profile link with customers after a service is rendered.

Can I reply to a review?

Yes. Choose the business-owner option when replying and write a respectful response. The platform is intended to email the complainant about a business reply.

Can I remove a review I disagree with?

Business owners can respond. An administrator investigates reports and decides whether content should be hidden or removed.

Ready to create your profile?

Start with your business details, then wait for approval before sharing your profile link.
