How CheckReport works

Find a service provider, share an honest account of your experience, and give the business a chance to respond. CheckReport is designed to make customer feedback easier to find and easier to act on.

01

Find a provider

Search by business name, website, social handle, or WhatsApp number. Read the experiences customers have shared.

02

Write your review

Tell us what service you bought, what happened, whether you tried to resolve it, and give a star rating. No photo upload is needed.

03

Choose how you appear

Use your name or post anonymously. Add your location so readers understand where the service took place.

04

Follow the conversation

Create a password after submitting to check your complaint activity. Businesses and other visitors can add a reply or comment.

What happens when you leave a review?

The form is split into two short steps, so you can explain the service first and choose your public display details separately.

1. Describe the service

  • Provider or business name and service bought
  • Optional website, social handle, and provider contact details if available
  • Whether you tried to resolve the issue directly
  • Your experience in detail and star rating

2. Add your update details

  • Your name, or choose anonymous display
  • Your location
  • Email and optional phone number for complaint updates
  • Create a password after submitting to sign in and follow your activity

Your update contacts stay private

Visitors can see your chosen display name (or Anonymous), location, star rating, and review details. Your email and phone number are for updates and are not displayed on the public review.

Replies and community comments

A visitor can identify themselves as the business owner or another customer, then write a reply or comment. A business reply is intended to notify the complainant by email. Replies should address the experience respectfully and avoid posting anyone’s private contact details.

Keep it factualDescribe what happened from your own point of view and avoid claims you cannot support.
Protect personal informationDo not include private phone numbers, email addresses, payment information, or identity documents.
Make room for resolutionShare whether you contacted the provider and what outcome would help resolve the issue.
